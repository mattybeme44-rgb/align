DSP validation

Synthetic signals with known answers, run through the same analyser, averaging and matching code the app measures with. Nothing on this page is a live microphone measurement — it exists so the maths can be checked on its own.

What cannot be tested here

  • Four-channel capture on your interface. The browser decides how many channels it will hand over. Open the app, press Multi-mic → Start listening and read the status line: it reports the device, the channels actually received and the sample rate.
  • Channel independence. The engine compares the inputs while running and reports when several of them carry the same signal. Patch a different signal into each channel to confirm it.
  • Clock and buffer stability. Late audio callbacks are counted during a live measurement and reported in the multi-microphone status line.
  • Microphone matching against real microphones. The arithmetic is checked below with a known difference; whether two capsules really agree is a measurement, not a test.
